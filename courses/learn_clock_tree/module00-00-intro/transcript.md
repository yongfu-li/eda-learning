Placement gave you cell centers. Clock tree synthesis connects a single source to every sink with controlled latency and skew.

CTS builds a tree from CLK to sinks A–F on our twelve-by-eight toy. You will measure skew, grow H-trees and MMM merges, balance with a zero-skew meeting point, then insert buffers.
